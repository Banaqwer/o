# Reaction statistics on real futures bars (seen period, not evidence)

First run on 9 November 2026; re-measured on 10 November 2026 after the
pipeline audit (protocol amendment 6, item 2). Data: London Strategic Edge
`/vault/candles`, 1-minute continuous front-month candles with volume,
`NASDAQ100.F` and `GOLD.F`, 2025-11-02 to 2026-11-08 (358,320 and 351,291
bars). Daily context from FRED (VIX, 10y-2y) and the French library. Engine
defaults, no parameter tuned to this data.

This year was looked at before the research protocol was written, so
nothing here is evidence of an edge; the protocol's development,
validation and hold-out results are in `docs/results.md`.

**Measurement.** Reaction is in sigma units of the dispersion `rho` at the
level test. MFE is the maximum move in the rung's direction within the
horizon, MAE the maximum move against it, edge = MFE - MAE, "reverted" the
share of events after which price revisited the session anchor. All of it
is measured from the open of the bar after the event, the first price
obtainable once the event is known. The first version of this document
measured from the level price itself, which credited the test bar's own
rejection as reaction and overstated the edge about threefold (NQ entries
at 15 bars: +1.59 then, +0.49 now; GC entries +0.35 then, -0.07 now). The
audit that found this is summarised in `docs/results.md`.

Measured from the next open, gold shows no reversion at its levels:
entries are indistinguishable from zero at every horizon and all tests are
slightly negative (-0.27 at 60 bars, t -2.1). The one-sided pattern
(selling rungs positive, buying rungs negative over the hour) is the
signature of a drifting market, not of two-sided reversion.
The split sample is flat to negative (first half -0.01 at 15 bars, second
half -0.21, t -2.5).

## How to read this

* Excursion statistics, not P&L. MFE - MAE is not a return a trader could
  have captured; no entry rule, exit rule or cost is modelled here. The
  costed overlay used by the protocol enters at the next open with a stop
  and a target and is the only number the research verdict rests on.
* Events overlap in time and within sessions, so the t-statistics overstate
  independence. The protocol uses session-level block-bootstrap inference
  for that reason.
* One year of one continuous series per instrument, from a single vendor.
* The thresholds that define an entry are rolling quantiles computed from
  past events only; nothing in the run uses future bars (a strict
  prefix-replay test on three weeks of real bars found zero mismatches).

Regenerate with:

```bash
atm-engine fetch-lse --api vault --symbol NQ --timeframe 1m --start 2025-11-02 --end 2026-11-09 --out nq_1m.csv
atm-engine context --start 2015-01-01 --out context.csv
atm-engine replay nq_1m.csv --instrument NQ --context context.csv --out nq_log
atm-engine stats nq_log --instrument NQ
```
